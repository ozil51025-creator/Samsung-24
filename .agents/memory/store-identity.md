For this project, the user wants their own branding and selected rewards tied to real product sales instead of daily investment returns. They previously proposed a 25% daily earning claim, but selected a sales-rewards alternative. The provided tier amounts are in UGX; full product names and cash-bonus terms remain unspecified. They requested overview, products, purchased products, referral, and account pages, plus a UGX 5,000 welcome bonus as withdrawable cash. They later specified referral bonus rates of 26%, 2%, and 1% for levels 1–3; the calculation base and qualifying event remain undefined.

**Why:** The user chose the sales-rewards option after the fixed daily return could not be verified and then requested member pages, a cash welcome bonus, real checkout, and multi-level referral bonuses.

**How to apply:** Use the user's brand rather than the reference site's identity. Keep all rewards inactive until the sale basis, eligibility, and payout terms are clear and verified; do not reuse the 25%-per-day investment claim or credit cash bonuses before those rules are configured.
