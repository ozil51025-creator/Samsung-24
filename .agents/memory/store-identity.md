---
name: Rewards site scope
description: The user's business model direction and branding for this website.
---

For this project, the user wants their own branding and selected rewards tied to real product sales instead of daily investment returns. They previously proposed a 25% daily earning claim, but selected a sales-rewards alternative. The provided tier amounts are in UGX; full product names and cash-bonus terms remain unspecified. They requested Overview, Products, Purchased, Referral, and Account pages, plus a UGX 5,000 welcome bonus as withdrawable cash. They later specified referral bonus rates of 26%, 2%, and 1% for levels 1–3; the calculation base and qualifying event remain undefined.

**Why:** The user chose the sales-rewards option after the fixed daily return could not be verified, then explicitly confirmed the product catalog should show sale prices only after sharing daily/hourly return calculations. They also requested member pages, a cash welcome bonus, real checkout, and multi-level referral bonuses. They corrected a preview that showed only three unlabeled icons instead of the five pages.

**How to apply:** Use the user's brand rather than the reference site's identity. Keep persistent mobile and desktop navigation labeled Overview, Products, Purchased, Referral, and Account; do not reduce it to icon-only shortcuts. Keep the Products catalog to sale prices; hide daily/hourly return figures. Keep rewards inactive until sale basis, eligibility, and payout terms are clear and verified; do not credit cash bonuses before rules are configured.

The user also chose a separate UGX wallet with customer top-ups and withdrawals, and named PesaJet as the desired gateway. PesaJet was not available in the current Replit integration search, so the Home/Overview wallet UI keeps actions disabled and totals/history unavailable rather than inventing transactions.

**Why:** The user wants actual money movement, but there is no connected wallet provider or transaction ledger; mock balances would mislead customers.

**How to apply:** Keep wallet actions disabled until a provider that supports UGX collections and Uganda payouts is available and the app can show verified, server-backed wallet data.
